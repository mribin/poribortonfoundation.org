ABOUT US

Creating lasting social change, one thread at a time

Poriborton Foundation is a Bangladesh-based registered NGO (Reg. No. S-14289/2025) working in poverty alleviation, education, healthcare, and community empowerment.

OUR MISSION

Sustainable change, community by community

To create sustainable social change through comprehensive programs in poverty alleviation, education, healthcare, and community empowerment across Bangladesh.

OUR VISION

A Bangladesh where everyone has what they need to grow

A Bangladesh where every individual has access to basic necessities, quality education, healthcare, and opportunities for growth and development.

OUR JOURNEY

A running stitch through the years

  1. 2010

    Foundation Established

    Poriborton Foundation was founded with a mission to create sustainable social change in Bangladesh.

  2. 2012

    First Education Program

    Launched our first school providing free education to 500 underprivileged children.

  3. 2015

    Healthcare Initiative

    Started mobile medical camps reaching remote villages with free healthcare services.

  4. 2017

    Disaster Relief Network

    Established a rapid-response team for emergency aid during natural disasters.

  5. 2019

    Community Empowerment

    Introduced vocational training and micro-loan programs for economic independence.

  6. 2023

    50,000+ Lives Impacted

    Reached a milestone of transforming over 50,000 lives across 200+ villages.

Note: this timeline (as published on our live site) says we were founded in 2010, while our homepage badge says "Trusted Since 2024" — we're aware of the mismatch and are getting it corrected.
