WHERE THE THREAD RUNS

Seven programs, one running stitch

Education

Primary, secondary, and higher-education support — scholarships, supplies, and nutrition — for children who'd otherwise be first to drop out.

Learn more →

Healthcare

Free surgeries, medical consultations, and medicine distribution for families who can't otherwise afford treatment.

Learn more →

Mosque Building

New construction, renovation, and expansion of mosques so communities have a dignified space to pray and gather.

Learn more →

Zakat Distribution

Shariah-compliant Zakat, delivered directly to verified eligible families with complete transparency.

Learn more →

Qurbani

Shariah-compliant Qurbani, distributed to families in Bangladesh and in crisis zones abroad each Eid al-Adha.

Skill Development

Vocational training and small-business support so families can build sustainable, independent livelihoods.
