WHAT WE DO

Seven programs, one running stitch

Comprehensive programs serving communities across Bangladesh with transparency and dedication.

PROGRAM 1

Education

Empowering students through comprehensive educational support from primary to higher education, ensuring every child has access to quality learning opportunities.

  • Primary Education — free education, supplies, and nutrition programs for young children in rural areas.
  • Secondary Education — supporting high school students with tuition, books, and exam preparation resources.
  • Higher Education — scholarships and financial support for university students pursuing professional degrees.
Donate to Education

PROGRAM 2

Healthcare

Delivering comprehensive healthcare services including surgeries, medical consultations, and free medicines to underserved communities nationwide.

  • Surgery & Operations — free surgical procedures and post-operative care for critical medical conditions.
  • Medical Services — comprehensive healthcare including consultations, diagnostics, and treatment programs.
  • Free Medicine Distribution — providing essential medicines and prescriptions to families unable to afford treatment.
Donate to Healthcare

PROGRAM 3

Mosque Building

Constructing and refurbishing mosques to provide dignified spaces for worship, Islamic education, and community services in underserved areas across Bangladesh.

  • New Construction — building new mosques in villages and communities without proper prayer facilities.
  • Renovation & Repair — refurbishing damaged mosques and upgrading facilities for growing congregations.
  • Expansion Projects — adding ablution facilities, prayer halls, and community spaces to existing mosques.
Donate to Mosque Building

PROGRAM 4

Zakat Distribution

Ensuring proper Islamic Zakat distribution to eligible recipients with complete transparency and Shariah compliance, reaching those most in need.

  • Zakat Distribution — direct cash assistance and support to verified Zakat-eligible families nationwide.
  • Eligible Recipients — identifying and verifying recipients according to Islamic principles and guidelines.
Give Zakat

PROGRAM 5

Qurbani

Organizing Shariah-compliant Qurbani services and distributing fresh meat to impoverished families during Eid al-Adha across Bangladesh and beyond.

  • Local Qurbani — performing and distributing Qurbani meat to needy families in Bangladesh.
  • International Qurbani — supporting crisis zones worldwide with Qurbani meat distribution programs.
Donate to Qurbani

PROGRAM 6

Skill Development

Providing vocational training and entrepreneurship programs to create sustainable livelihoods and economic independence for disadvantaged communities.

  • Vocational Training — teaching practical skills in sewing, carpentry, electronics, and other trades.
  • Entrepreneurship — business training, microfinance support, and mentorship for small business owners.
Donate to Skill Development

PROGRAM 7

Disaster Relief

Rapid emergency response providing food, shelter, medical care, and rehabilitation support to communities affected by natural disasters and crises.

  • Emergency Response — immediate relief operations during floods, cyclones, and other natural disasters.
  • Food Distribution — regular food assistance programs for vulnerable families facing food insecurity.
Donate to Disaster Relief
